What good is thievery if you've nowhere to go? -Greirat

Pilfer some trinkets and store them here. Better here than lost to the whims of execs.

Creativity boards are pretty rare, and captured by one particular company that is becoming increasingly hostile to its userbase. This is an attempt to self-host something similar.

The key goals for this app are:

1) Serendipity -- the ability to find valuable or agreeable things not sought for
2) Organization in the form of boards and mosaic feeds
3) Categorization via tagging and eventually smart tagging with machine learning

Once the backend is complete, I'll start work on a browser oriented frontend, as well as a tui, and perhaps a mobile app.
